## Introducing myself 

Hello! 
My name is Pia Sen, and I'm a 5th year PhD candidate at The George Washington University in Washington, DC. 
My research interests center on microbial eco-evolutionary processes (especially involving microbial communication), extremophiles, and astrobiology. I discovered research (and science in general) through synthetic biology, where I fell in love with the concept of "evolution as tinkering" (Jacobs, 1977). 
During my PhD, I've done a combination of field work, experimental cultivation/enrichment, and metagenomics. While not part of my dissertation, I also have experience with genetic techniques like cloning & gene knockout (targeted and non-targeted). I love benchwork, and I want to use metagenomics to guide my experimental exploration of microbial adaptations.  
As a postdoctoral researcher, I hope to gain skills in culture-independent microbial ecology and quantitative microscopy, but I'm open to learning other things that teach me how to think in new and unique ways.
